About us

Helping Indian shoppers find practical value

India Best Deals is an independent deal-discovery project for online shoppers in India. Our goal is to reduce the time people spend checking multiple stores while keeping the final purchase decision in their hands.

How offers are selected

We use approved feeds and retailer APIs to compare sale price with listed price. Automated checks remove repeated alerts and prioritize stronger discounts. We also encourage shoppers to verify warranty, seller, delivery and return conditions.

Editorial principles

  • We do not guarantee that every discount is the lowest historical price.
  • Affiliate commission does not increase the buyer's price.
  • Retailers control prices, stock, delivery and customer service.
  • We correct or remove inaccurate information when identified.
