Independent shopping guidance

Make the discount work for you

Buying electronics online

Compare the final checkout price, not only the headline discount. Check warranty coverage, seller ratings, replacement terms and whether bank offers require a specific card or EMI plan.

Reading a deal correctly

A large percentage off MRP is not always the lowest market price. Review the recent selling price, delivery charge, coupon conditions and product variant before purchasing.

Avoiding impulse purchases

Set a budget and a required-feature list first. A deal is valuable only when the product fits a real need and remains affordable after any interest or delivery charges.
